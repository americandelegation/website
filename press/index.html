About the show

Who we are.

The American Delegation is an independent Eurovision podcast and media brand. Hosts Stafford Jones and John D Carrion cover the contest for American fans, from the national selections to the Grand Final, and publish guides to watching and voting from home.

America doesn't compete in Eurovision. The show covers it the way an American delegation would if there were one, tracking the American connections in each year's field and writing for fans who watch from here. The American Delegation is published by AD Media, LLC.

Bios for print.

Stafford Jones

Stafford Jones co-hosts The American Delegation. He’s a brand marketer who has worked at Disney and Publicis Groupe, and he’s been hooked on Eurovision since 2022.

John D Carrion

John D Carrion co-hosts The American Delegation. He has been to seven Eurovision Song Contests since Lisbon 2018 and spends his working life with nonprofits and queer choirs.
