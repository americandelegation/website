Episode 04, out now

The Bangkok Debrief

A Eurovision Asia recap

On Saturday, November 14, eleven countries sang at the first Eurovision Asia. The Philippines won with Carmelle’s “Sobra-Sobra,” and one performance went to the voters as a rehearsal tape.

John D Carrion takes us through the night in Bangkok, and the arrivals board shows where every act landed. Then we open the diplomatic pouch to see how our Episode 02 predictions held up.

Show notes and sources

John takes us through the night at One Bangkok Forum, from the tuk-tuk entrances to the vote, and we ask whether Malaysia’s broadcaster should be allowed back after it cut away from a drag queen. Then the arrivals board shows where every act landed, and the pouch opens on the predictions we sealed in Episode 02. After two of the four, we’re tied at one point each.

Corrections

  • In Oslo in 2010, the stage invader interrupted Daniel Diges, and Spain sang again after the last act.
  • The EBU made Malta take “kant” out of “Serving” for the 2025 contest, so every broadcast heard the new version.
  • Voxovation was set up to adapt Eurovision for new regions under license from the EBU. We couldn’t confirm where it’s registered.
