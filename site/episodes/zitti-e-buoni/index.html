Zitti e buoni

Måneskin, Italy 2021

In 2017, Måneskin came second on X Factor Italia. Four years later they won Sanremo with “Zitti e buoni” and took the swearing out so Eurovision could air it. In Rotterdam the juries put them fourth, and Europe’s phones gave Italy its first win in 31 years.

John D Carrion takes us down the road to Rotterdam, and we ask whether we’d still be debating this song if Måneskin had never gotten famous. Then “Zitti e buoni” faces the five tests: is it a Eurovision Classic?
