Eurovision Asia 2026: results and how to rewatch

The Philippines won the first Eurovision Song Contest Asia in Bangkok. Here are the full results and how to rewatch the show from America.

Updated November 15, 2026 · 4 min read

The short answer
  • The Philippines won with CARMELLE’s “Sobra-Sobra”
  • 211 points, first with the juries and the public
  • Thailand came second and Vietnam third
  • Next stop for CARMELLE: a guest spot at Eurovision 2027 in Burgas

The results

CARMELLE, who’s 19, won for the Philippines with 211 points. She came first with the juries and first with the public, so there was no split to argue about at the top. Thailand, the host country, came second on the strength of its public vote.

How the night went

The Grand Final was on Saturday, November 14, at One Bangkok Forum, hosted by Woody Milintachinda and Cindy Bishop for Thailand’s Channel 3. The acts rode onto the stage in tuk-tuks, and every vocal was live. Jeff Satur and DARA, who won Eurovision for Bulgaria in May, performed while the votes came in.

One performance didn’t go to plan. Laos’s Thinla couldn’t hear herself through her in-ear monitor, so after the last song the broadcast showed her dress rehearsal instead. Laos finished fifth.

How the vote worked

Eleven national juries and twelve public votes counted: one for each country, plus a “Rest of the World” vote cast online from 172 countries and territories. Americans voted as part of the Rest of the World. Each voter got eight free votes to spread across at least three songs.

Where to rewatch

The Grand Final streamed live on the official Eurovision Song Contest YouTube channel, which is the place to look for the replay and the individual performances.

What’s next

The winner gets a guest spot at Eurovision 2027 in Burgas, where the Grand Final is on May 15. Our debrief from Bangkok is Episode 04, The Bangkok Debrief, out Thursday, November 19.
