How to watch Eurovision in America

Where it streams and when it starts here, with a watch party plan worth taking the afternoon off for.

Updated October 3, 2026  ·  4 min read

The short answer
  • May 11–15 2027 in Burgas, Bulgaria
  • Every show starts at 3 p.m. Eastern
  • In 2026, Americans watched on Peacock and Eurovision's YouTube channel
  • How 2027 will air in America hasn't been announced
In this guide
  1. When it's on
  2. Where to watch
  3. How long it runs
  4. Watch party tips

When Eurovision 2027 is on (American time zones)

Eurovision 2027 is in Burgas, Bulgaria, at Arena Burgas. All three live shows start at 21:00 Central European time, which is 3 p.m. Eastern and noon Pacific.

ShowDateEasternPacific
Semi-final 1Tue, May 11, 20273 p.m.Noon
Semi-final 2Thu, May 13, 20273 p.m.Noon
Grand FinalSat, May 15, 20273 p.m.Noon

Yes, the semi-finals air during the American workday.

Where to watch

How Eurovision 2027 will air in America hasn't been announced yet. Here's how it worked for Eurovision 2026 in Vienna, which is the best guide to what to expect:

  • Peacock streamed all three live shows in America.
  • The official Eurovision Song Contest YouTube channel also streamed the live shows to viewers in America and other countries without a broadcaster.

We'll update this guide as soon as we know how Burgas will air in America.

How long each broadcast lasts

The semi-finals usually run a little over two hours. The Grand Final is closer to four, including the vote, which is often the best part.

Planning a watch party

  • Make it the Grand Final. A Saturday at 3 p.m. Eastern is about as good a slot as an American Eurovision party gets.
  • Watch the entries first. The songs are released weeks ahead, so your guests can pick favorites before the show.
  • Vote together. Yes, Americans can. Here's how.
  • Score it yourself. Hand out scorecards with Eurovision's points: 1 to 8, 10 and 12.
