Why we started this

Eurovision deserves better than once a year.

We've always thought Eurovision was worth more than the “look how weird Europe is” treatment it usually gets over here.

Every May we'd watch the Grand Final, then spend the rest of the year piecing the story together from European fan sites written for people who had already seen every national final. So we started the show we wanted to hear, one that knows the contest well and isn't embarrassed to love it.

We'll still argue about staging and the occasional baffling broadcaster decision, the way fans do.

Want to come on the show or work with us? We'd love to hear from you.
